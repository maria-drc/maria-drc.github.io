# About Me

## Academic life

Before joining the [Complexity Science Hub](https://www.csh.ac.at/) as a [JSMF](https://www.jsmf.org/) Research Fellow, I did my PhD in Mathematics at [INET, Oxford](http://www.inet.ox.ac.uk/). I was supervised by [J. Doyne Farmer](http://www.doynefarmer.com/about-me), [François Lafond](https://francoislafond.info/) and [Mariano Beguerrisse-Diaz](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/beguerisse/).

My PhD thesis focused on developing a data-driven network model of the labour market to understand the impact of automation and COVID-19 on employment. I also studied global financial contagion in multilayer networks when I was a research intern at the [International Monetary Fund](https://www.imf.org/en/home).

Before starting my PhD, I did my BSc in Physics at [Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México](https://www.unam.mx/) (UNAM) 2011-2016. I was a research intern at Imperial College London, where we worked on trade networks, and at Ryerson University, where we studied the robot crawler problem. I also had a taste of the world outside academia when I worked as a data scientist: One summer in Inno-ba and then a few months in [Pondera](http://ponderalab.com.mx/quienes-somos/).

## Hobbies

When I am not coding, studying or getting my hands dirty with some pen and paper, I love doing sports and enjoy having dinner or drinks with friends and coworkers. Occasionally, I’ll be dancing Salsa on a Friday night.

Football is my favourite sport. I played for the Oxford University’s Futsal team and for the 2nds Football team. I also enjoy a casual table tennis game and organising sport events for [Balliol College MCR](https://balliolmcr.com/) (I was MCR sport officer 2017-2018 and 2019-2020).
